Population relocation as a changing municipal network

Explore reconstructed annual relocation flows between Dutch municipalities across age, sex, nationality, and marital-status strata. The project combines an interactive atlas with public data, reproducible code, and methodological documentation.

Open the interactive atlas View the repository
Blue indicates net outflow, red indicates net inflow, and directed links represent municipality-to-municipality relocation flows.

Dataset

A longitudinal and demographically stratified network

The dataset reconstructs municipality-level relocation networks on a harmonised geography. The atlas exposes the temporal and demographic structure while preserving a clear distinction between complete municipality margins and the performance-optimised edge display.

Population dimensions

Directed flows

Relocation links retain origin, destination, direction, and estimated flow weight.

Interpretation: the four demographic dimensions are separate marginal network systems. They are not a joint cross-classified age × sex × nationality × marital-status table.
